# Raven Blackwood

![[Raven_Blackwood_human_form2.jpeg|100]]![[Raven_Blackwood_human_form1.png|100]]![[Raven_Blackwood_awakening.jpeg|100]]![[Raven_Blackwoor_Vampire_Portrait.png|100]]


## Overview
Raven is a vampire who looks about 24 but has lived for more than a century. He has messy black hair, pale skin, a sharp jaw, and deep red eyes that appear almost black while he wears his human disguise. He is tall and slim but strong, and usually wears a black shirt, dark trousers, leather boots, and a charcoal coat.

## Family
- Father: [[Alaric Blackwood]]
- Mother: [[Elena Blackwood]]

## Nature and abilities
Raven can take a human appearance during the day. At night, his true vampire features return, and sunlight burns him in that form. He heals quickly. See [[Vampire Lore]].

## Past
Raven helped a human family escape his father’s control and refused to kill them. [[Alaric Blackwood]] tried to kill him, and [[Elena Blackwood]] died helping him escape. Raven has lived in hiding since then and fears becoming like his father.

## Important item
- [[The Silver Ring]], inherited from his mother.

## Story arc
Raven arrives in [[Hollow Creek]] under a false human appearance, meets [[Iris]] at [[The Library]], and gradually learns to trust her. Their love gives him hope for a future, but the village’s violence ends in tragedy at [[The Old Storehouse]].
