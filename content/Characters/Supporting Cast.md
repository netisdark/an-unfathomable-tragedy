# Supporting Cast

- [[Mr Vale]]: the elderly librarian at [[The Library]].
- [[Mrs Whitmore]]: the elderly widow who runs [[The Boarding House]] and rents Raven a room.
- [[Alaric Blackwood]]: Raven’s cruel father, who believes humans should serve vampires.
- [[Elena Blackwood]]: Raven’s mother, who believes humans and vampires can coexist.
- [[Margaret Miller]]: Iris’s grandmother, who encouraged her curiosity and left her a pendant and old books.
- [[Thomas Miller]]: Iris’s father and a carpenter.
- [[Clara Miller]]: Iris’s mother and a seamstress.
- [[William Miller]]: Iris’s older brother, who left for the capital.
