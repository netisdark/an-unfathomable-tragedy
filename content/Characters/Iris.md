# Iris Miller

![[Iris_miller.png|100]]![[Iris_miller2.jpeg|100]]

## Overview
Iris is 21 and lives in [[Hollow Creek]]. She has long dark-brown hair, warm brown eyes, and a small scar above her left eyebrow. She is curious, witty, compassionate, brave, stubborn, and independent. Her flaws include recklessness and pride, and she often helps others before thinking about her own safety.

## Family
- Grandmother: [[Margaret Miller]]
- Father: [[Thomas Miller]]
- Mother: [[Clara Miller]]
- Older brother: [[William Miller]]

## Personal items
- [[Margaret’s Pendant]], a silver pendant inherited from her grandmother.

## Life and goals
Iris works part-time at [[The Library]] and wants to understand the world beyond her village. Her grandmother encouraged her to read and ask questions. Iris should remain an active character with her own choices and goals, not merely a love interest.

## Story arc
She begins curious about the stranger in town, becomes Raven’s partner, learns he is a vampire, and chooses to trust him despite her fear. The village turns against her, leading to the tragedy at [[The Old Storehouse]].
