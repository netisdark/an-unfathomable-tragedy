# An Unfathomable Tragedy

This vault contains all eight drafted chapters, character profiles, family background, setting pages, vampire lore, continuity notes, and image-reference links.

## Internal links
Story chapters use Obsidian `[[wikilinks]]` for characters, family members, important places, and lore. Click a linked name or place to open its background page. If you want a link to show the exact wording in the story, the vault uses aliases such as `[[Raven Blackwood|raven]]`.

## Picture references
Open [[Image References]] for curated Unsplash, Pexels, and Pinterest search links for character portraits, family, settings, and key scenes. These are links to image collections, not included image files; download the pictures you like into `Images/` and check usage rights.

The chapters are a complete first draft and can be expanded or revised later.
