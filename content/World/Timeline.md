# timeline
1. raven is born into the Blackwood family.
2. elena teaches raven that humans and vampires can coexist.
3. raven helps a human family escape alaric.
4. alaric orders raven to kill the family; raven refuses.
5. alaric tries to kill raven, and elena dies helping him escape.
6. raven lives alone for years.
7. raven enters hollow creek and rents a room.
8. raven meets iris at the library.
9. they become friends and partners in helping people.
10. iris discovers raven is a vampire.
11. their friendship becomes love.
12. villagers learn iris supports raven and turn against her.
13. iris is captured and imprisoned in the storehouse.
14. the villagers set the storehouse on fire; flames spread.
15. iris saves raven from a falling beam and is fatally injured.
16. iris dies before their planned escape becomes a new life.
17. raven leaves hollow creek carrying her pendant.

## chronology note
the exact historical period, raven's birth year, and precise age are not fixed. keep exact dates out until the setting is chosen.
