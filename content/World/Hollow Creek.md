## Hollow Creek and its buildings
![[hollow_creek1.jpeg|150]]![[hollow_creek_old_wooden_house_interior.jpeg|100]]![[hollow_creek_stree.jpeg|100]]

a small, old-fashioned village near a dark forest, with stone houses, wooden shops, a public square, a church, a bakery, a boarding house, and an old library.

## locations
- **boarding house:** run by mrs. whitmore; raven rents a room here.
- **library:** where raven and iris meet.
- **public square:** announcements, gossip, and gatherings.
- **Blackwood house:** a large, shadowy home near the forest.
- **old storehouse:** where iris is imprisoned and where the fire begins.
- **northern road:** the route raven and iris intend to take.

## the fire
the villagers set fire to the storehouse where iris is held. dry timber and supplies nearby help the flames spread, and wind carries embers to nearby roofs. the fire destroys much of the village and kills many people.
