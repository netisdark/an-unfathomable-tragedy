# vampire lore
villagers say vampires have pale skin, sharp teeth, red eyes, long ears, no sympathy for humans, and prey on weaker beings. they believe vampires are demons and bring bad luck. some fears come from real attacks, but villagers wrongly treat all vampires alike.

## raven's abilities
- can take on a human appearance during the day.
- true form returns at night, usually by midnight.
- true form has pale skin, red eyes, longer ears, and sharp teeth.
- sunlight burns him in true form.
- he heals quickly.

## open questions for revisions

- define whether vampires need blood and how raven avoids harming humans.
- decide whether raven's day-human ability is rare because of his mother's side.

## thematic rule
being a vampire does not automatically make someone evil. characters are responsible for their choices.
