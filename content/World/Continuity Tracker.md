# continuity tracker

## confirmed
- raven can imitate human appearance during the day; his true form returns at night.
- sunlight burns him in his true form.
- raven keeps a silver ring from his mother.
- alaric believes humans exist to serve vampires.
- iris is 21 and works part-time at the library.
- iris wears a silver pendant from her grandmother.
- romance develops gradually through teasing, arguments, friendship, and tenderness.
- villagers' fear is partly based on real danger but becomes superstition and collective violence.
- iris dies after helping raven escape the fire.
- raven leaves alone with iris's pendant.
