# family history

## the Blackwoods
- **alaric blackwood:** powerful, controlling, convinced vampires should rule humans.
- **elena blackwood:** compassionate and convinced coexistence is possible.
- **raven blackwood:** their only child. he helps a human family escape, provoking alaric's wrath.

## the Millers
- **thomas and clara miller:** iris's parents; he is a carpenter and she is a seamstress.
- **iris miller:** their curious and stubborn daughter, who works at the library.
- **william miller:** iris's older brother, who left for the capital.
- **margaret miller:** iris's late grandmother, who fostered her love of books and left her a silver pendant.
