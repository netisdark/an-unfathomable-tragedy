# chapter seven: the last escape

[[Raven Blackwood|raven]] struck the [[The Old Storehouse|storehouse]] door with his shoulder. the wood groaned but did not open. inside, [[Iris|iris]] coughed and pulled against the ropes. smoke gathered near the ceiling, and a burning beam dropped somewhere behind her.

"move away from the door!" [[Raven Blackwood|raven]] shouted.

she dragged herself as far from it as the rope allowed. [[Raven Blackwood|raven]] struck the door again. this time, the frame split, and he pushed through the opening. his true form was exposed to the night. his red eyes caught the firelight, and the villagers nearby stumbled away in terror.

"the demon!" someone screamed.

[[Raven Blackwood|raven]] ignored them. he found [[Iris|iris]] tied to the post. for one terrible second, all he could see was her face, pale beneath the soot.

"you came," she whispered.

"of course i came."

"you are late."

"i know."

he tore at the knot. the rope had tightened from her struggling, and his hands shook. a burning piece of timber fell nearby.

"[[Raven Blackwood|raven]]," [[Iris|iris]] coughed. "the rope."

"i'm trying."

"try harder."

he finally freed her. she stumbled into him, and he wrapped his coat around her shoulders.

"can you walk?"

"yes."

"are you certain?"

"no. but i can complain while we try."

he guided her through the broken doorway. outside, villagers scattered. harrow backed away from [[Raven Blackwood|raven]]'s red eyes and sharp teeth. [[Raven Blackwood|raven]] could have chased him, but [[Iris|iris]] gripped his sleeve, and getting her out was all that mattered.

they ran through the village. behind them, the [[The Old Storehouse|storehouse]] roof groaned. flames reached the dry timber stacked along the wall. the wind carried sparks to a shed, then to a haystack. soon the fire was no longer contained.

people shouted for water. buckets passed from hand to hand, but the flames moved faster. a burning roof collapsed into the lane, blocking the shortest route to the northern road.

[[Raven Blackwood|raven]] turned toward a narrow passage between two houses. smoke filled it, and [[Iris|iris]] stumbled. he caught her before she fell. when they emerged, they found a group of villagers trapped near a burning cart, including the boy who had warned [[Raven Blackwood|raven]]. [[Raven Blackwood|raven]] looked at [[Iris|iris]].

she nodded.

together, they helped the boy and two older women climb over a low stone wall. one woman stared at [[Raven Blackwood|raven]] in horror even as he lifted her to safety. he said nothing and made sure she reached the other side.

they kept moving. sparks drifted like burning snow. people ran in every direction, calling names and carrying children. some reached the fields. others were cut off by falling beams and smoke. the disaster had grown beyond anyone's control.

[[Raven Blackwood|raven]] and [[Iris|iris]] reached the lane that led out of the village. the road was nearly clear, and the darkness beyond it promised safety.

then a loud crack split the air. a beam from a nearby building fell across the road, sending burning fragments toward them. [[Raven Blackwood|raven]] turned to shield [[Iris|iris]], but the beam was falling toward him and he had no time to move.

[[Iris|iris]] shoved him with all her strength.

he stumbled out of its path.

the beam struck the ground. [[Raven Blackwood|raven]] turned and saw [[Iris|iris]] lying beside the fallen timber.

he dropped to his knees. "[[Iris|iris]]?"

she tried to sit up, but a sharp breath escaped her. blood darkened the side of her blouse. the beam had struck her as she pushed him away.

[[Raven Blackwood|raven]] pressed his hands against the wound. "we have to go. i can carry you."

"[[Raven Blackwood|raven]]..."

"don't speak. save your strength."

he lifted her carefully and carried her beyond the last houses, across the open field, until the heat of the fire began to fade behind them. he did not stop until they reached the old stone bridge.

there he lowered her onto the grass.

"we're out," he said. "[[Iris|iris]], we're out. you're safe now."

she looked at him, and the sadness in her eyes frightened him more than the fire.

"[[Raven Blackwood|raven]]," she whispered. "i don't think i am."

"no." he pressed his hands harder against the wound. "you are going to be fine. i'll find a doctor. i'll carry you to the next town."

she reached for his wrist.

"please stop."

he shook his head. "i can save you."

"you don't have to save everyone."

"i have to save you."

the words broke as they left his mouth.

[[Iris|iris]] looked at him for a long moment, then touched his cheek.

"you came back for me," she said.

"i was late."

"you came."

[[Raven Blackwood|raven]] lowered his forehead to hers. the village they had known was disappearing into smoke behind them. he had lived for more than a century, and believed that if he kept moving, he could survive anything.

now he would have given every year of his long life for one more hour with her.

"stay with me," he whispered.

[[Iris|iris]] tried to smile.

"i am trying."

and [[Raven Blackwood|raven]], who had never feared death for himself, finally understood what it meant to be afraid of losing someone else.
