# chapter five: the town's enemy

the first rumor began at the [[The Bakery|bakery]]. someone had seen [[Iris|iris]] leaving the old stone building near [[The Forest|the forest]] after sunset. by the following morning, the story had changed. [[Iris|iris]] was no longer simply meeting a strange man. she was helping a vampire enter [[Hollow Creek|hollow creek]].

by the end of the week, people said she had stolen books to teach [[Vampire Lore|vampires]] the secrets of the village. one woman swore her milk had turned sour because [[Iris|iris]] had passed her house.

[[Iris|iris]] tried to laugh.

"apparently i have become very powerful," she told [[Raven Blackwood|raven]]. "i can spoil milk just by walking down the street."

[[Raven Blackwood|raven]] did not laugh. they sat at the back of [[The Library|the library]], where the shelves kept their voices from carrying.

"they will not stop with rumors," he said.

"then we tell them the truth."

"which truth? that i'm a vampire? they already believe the worst."

"so we let them decide who i am, too?"

"i'm trying to keep you safe."

"by deciding what i'm allowed to know and do? you sound like my father."

[[Raven Blackwood|raven]] flinched. [[Iris|iris]] regretted the words but did not take them back.

"i don't want to hide you," she said more quietly. "i want people to understand you haven't hurt them."

"some [[Vampire Lore|vampires]] have."

"i know."

"some might again."

"i know that too, [[Raven Blackwood|raven]]. i'm asking them to judge the person standing in front of them."

the next day, [[Iris|iris]] spoke at the village council gathering in the [[The Public Square|public square]]. she admitted that [[Raven Blackwood|raven]] was a vampire and that [[Vampire Lore|vampires]] could be dangerous. then she asked why fear gave the village the right to condemn someone before he had harmed them.

a council leader named harrow stepped forward. years earlier, he had lost a cousin to a vampire attack, and his grief had hardened into certainty.

"you defend a demon," he said.

"i defend a person who has shown me kindness."

"he has bewitched you."

"no. he has disagreed with me, annoyed me, and made me shelve books until my arms hurt. if that is a spell, it is badly designed."

a few people shifted uncomfortably.

harrow's face darkened. "you have chosen your side."

"i have chosen not to hate someone because it is convenient."

she left before anyone could stop her.

that evening, her parents begged her to be careful. [[Thomas Miller|thomas]] feared for her safety. [[Clara Miller|clara]] wanted her daughter to leave town until the rumors passed.

"you don't have to save everyone," her mother said.

"i know."

"then why does it always feel as if you're trying?"

[[Iris|iris]] had no answer.

later, she found [[Raven Blackwood|raven]] waiting in the lane behind the house.

"you shouldn't have done that," he told her.

"hello to you, too."

"they know now."

"they already suspected."

"now they have a reason to come after you."

[[Iris|iris]] folded her arms. "i won't apologize for telling the truth."

"i want you to be alive."

the words came out so quietly that she almost missed them.

she stepped closer and took his hand. his fingers were cold.

"i am afraid," she admitted. "not of you. of what they might do. but i won't let them decide that loving someone makes me wicked."

he looked at her as if she had spoken in a language he had forgotten.

"there is a place beyond the northern road," he said. "a town where no one knows my family. we could leave."

"together?"

"if you still want to."

she smiled, though worry remained in it. "i will need to bring my books."

"of course."

"and you will have to carry them."

"i expected as much."

they planned to leave after the next market day, when the roads would be busy. [[Raven Blackwood|raven]] would find work. [[Iris|iris]] would find a [[The Library|library]], or make one if she had to. they would live somewhere quiet, where the past could not follow so easily.

for the first time, the future seemed real.

but [[Hollow Creek|hollow creek]] had already begun to turn against them. and fear, once given a crowd to hide inside, was becoming something far more dangerous than a rumor.
