# chapter four: the truth between them

[[Iris|iris]] began to notice that [[Raven Blackwood|raven]] disappeared whenever evening grew late. he would walk with her through the market, help her carry books, and argue about whether a person could survive on bread alone. then, as the light faded, he would suddenly remember somewhere else he needed to be.

one evening, she followed him. she told herself it was only to prove he was hiding something ridiculous. perhaps he had a secret sweetheart. perhaps he was part of an illegal book club. perhaps he was a very bad spy.

[[Raven Blackwood|raven]] left the main road and entered an old stone building at the edge of the village. [[Iris|iris]] waited, then crept closer. she heard a strained sound and the scrape of furniture. she pushed the door open.

[[Raven Blackwood|raven]] stood in the middle of the room, one hand braced against a table. his disguise was fading. his skin had turned pale, his ears were growing longer, and his eyes glowed red. sharp teeth showed when he looked up.

for a moment, neither moved.

[[Iris|iris]] felt the floor drop beneath her. all the stories she had heard as a child rushed back into her mind.

[[Raven Blackwood|raven]] took one step toward her.

"don't come near me," she said.

he stopped. "i wasn't going to hurt you."

"you lied to me."

"i never told you i was human."

"you let me believe it!"

he had no answer.

"how long have you been a vampire?"

"all my life."

"and all those times we walked together, all those books, all those conversations... was any of it real?"

"yes."

"how am i supposed to believe that?"

"you shouldn't have to believe me just because i say it."

[[Iris|iris]]'s hand closed around the [[Margaret’s Pendant|pendant]] at her throat. [[Raven Blackwood|raven]] saw the movement and looked away.

"have you killed anyone?" she asked.

his silence lasted too long. "i have hurt people. i've done things i regret. but no human has died by my hand."

[[Iris|iris]] searched his face for a lie. she found fear, shame, and something that looked painfully like honesty. but she was still afraid.

"i need you to leave."

[[Raven Blackwood|raven]] nodded once. he did not follow her when she ran.

for several days, [[Iris|iris]] avoided [[The Library|the library]] during the hours he usually visited. she told herself she was being sensible. yet she remembered the boy accused of stealing an apple, the widow who had been mocked, and the books carried through the rain. none of those things had been an illusion.

on the fourth day, she found [[Raven Blackwood|raven]] outside [[The Library|the library]]. he held a book beneath his coat.

"i thought you might need this," he said. "you mentioned your grandmother's books referred to a history of [[The Forest|the forest]]. this one has a section about the Blackwood family."

"why are you giving it to me?"

"because you wanted to know."

"and you aren't afraid i'll tell everyone what you are?"

"i am afraid."

his honesty caught her off guard.

"then why come here?"

"because i'd rather tell you the truth than spend the rest of my life pretending our friendship meant nothing."

[[Iris|iris]] took the book. "i'm still frightened of you."

"i know."

"i don't know if i can trust you."

"i know that too."

"you are very annoying when you agree with everything i say."

"i can disagree if it would help."

despite herself, [[Iris|iris]] almost smiled. she hated that he could still make her want to smile.

"i need time," she said.

"take all you need."

she turned toward [[The Library|the library]] door, then glanced back. "you can come inside. but you're shelving the books today."

"all of them?"

"every single one."

"you are cruel, [[Iris|iris]] miller."

"and you are a vampire. we all have our flaws."

this time, [[Raven Blackwood|raven]] laughed. it was a small, unfamiliar sound, but [[Iris|iris]] felt something in her chest loosen.

she was not ready to stop being afraid. trust would take time, and love was still far away. but she had chosen to know the truth instead of letting fear choose for her.

[[Raven Blackwood|raven]], who had spent years believing the truth would always make people leave, followed her into [[The Library|the library]].
