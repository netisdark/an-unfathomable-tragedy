# chapter six: the night of fire

the day before their planned escape, [[Iris|iris]] went to [[The Library|the library]] to return the keys and gather the few belongings she had left there. [[Mr Vale|mr. vale]] stood beside the desk, twisting a piece of string around his fingers.

"you should leave town tonight," he said.

"we're leaving tomorrow."

"tomorrow may be too late."

he told her that harrow had been meeting with men from the northern farms. they called it a cleansing. they intended to make an example of the girl who defended a vampire.

[[Iris|iris]] hurried home, but groups of villagers stood together in the streets. when she passed, conversations stopped. a boy who had once borrowed books from her looked down at his shoes.

her mother had packed a small bag. her father stood by the door.

"we are going to your aunt's house," [[Clara Miller|clara]] said. "now."

"i have to find [[Raven Blackwood|raven]]."

"[[Iris|iris]], please."

"if they are looking for him, he needs to know."

[[Thomas Miller|thomas]] caught her by the shoulders. "you cannot help him if you are dead."

"and i cannot live with myself if i leave him alone."

her father embraced her fiercely. "you have always been too much like your grandmother."

[[Iris|iris]] kissed his cheek and slipped out the back door.

she never reached the lane. three men were waiting near the old well. they seized her arms. she kicked one in the shin and twisted free, but another caught her bag and dragged her back. they tied her wrists with rope and pulled her through the streets.

"stop struggling!" one shouted.

"then stop kidnapping people!"

villagers watched from doorways. some looked away. others followed, telling themselves they were protecting their homes.

they took her to an old [[The Old Storehouse|storehouse]] near the village edge. it had once held grain, timber, and tools. now dry boards were stacked against one wall, and bundles of straw lay beneath broken windows.

harrow stood outside with a lantern.

"you have been given every chance to turn away from this creature," he said.

"his name is [[Raven Blackwood|raven]]."

"you have chosen a demon over your own people."

"you have chosen to tie up a woman and burn her alive. tell me which of us is acting like a monster."

his face tightened. he ordered the men to lock her inside.

they bound [[Iris|iris]] to a thick post. the rope cut into her wrists. she pulled until her hands hurt, but the knot held. outside, villagers argued. some wanted to burn the [[The Old Storehouse|storehouse]] immediately. others looked frightened, but none had the courage to stop it.

[[Iris|iris]] tried to steady her breathing. she thought of her mother, her father, [[The Library|the library]], and the house she and [[Raven Blackwood|raven]] had imagined.

"you had better come," she whispered. "i am going to be furious if you make me wait."

far from the [[The Old Storehouse|storehouse]], [[Raven Blackwood|raven]] was preparing their escape. he had packed food, money, a map, and the books [[Iris|iris]] insisted on bringing. then a young boy arrived at the [[The Boarding House|boarding house]], breathless and pale.

"they took her," he said. "[[Iris|iris]]. they took her to the old [[The Old Storehouse|storehouse]]. they're bringing firewood."

[[Raven Blackwood|raven]] ran.

he reached a corner where he could see the crowd near the [[The Old Storehouse|storehouse]]. he could change into his true form and move faster, but the village was full of people. if they saw him, they would know.

he hesitated for one terrible moment, still hoping to free [[Iris|iris]] without revealing himself.

then he heard a shout.

the delay had already cost him time.

[[Raven Blackwood|raven]] pulled off his gloves and let the disguise fall away. his skin turned pale, his eyes burned red, and his teeth sharpened. he ran toward the [[The Old Storehouse|storehouse]] without hiding.

outside, harrow lifted a torch. the dry wood caught quickly, and flames climbed the wall. some villagers cheered. others stared in silence.

inside, [[Iris|iris]] smelled smoke and pulled against the ropes. she shouted for help, but the crackling fire swallowed her voice.

then something crashed against the door.

"[[Iris|iris]]!"

[[Raven Blackwood|raven]]'s voice.

"i'm here!" she shouted. "and when you get me out, i am going to tell you exactly what i think of your timing!"

a broken laugh escaped him from the other side.

"i deserve that."

the door shook again, and the fire climbed higher.
