# chapter one: the stranger in town

[[Vampire Lore|vampires]] were creatures people feared.

they were said to have pale skin, sharp teeth, red eyes, and long ears. they lived in dark forests, far from human settlements, and preyed on those weaker than themselves. according to the stories told by elders, [[Vampire Lore|vampires]] had no sympathy for humans. they saw them as nothing more than food.

parents told their children to stay away from the woods after sunset. travelers avoided lonely roads at night. whenever someone suspected a vampire was nearby, doors were locked, windows were shut, and prayers were whispered behind closed walls.

in [[Hollow Creek|hollow creek]], people believed [[Vampire Lore|vampires]] were more than dangerous creatures. they believed they were demons, bringers of bad luck, and a threat to every human soul.

but not every story told the whole truth.

deep inside [[The Forest|the forest]] lived a vampire named [[Raven Blackwood|raven blackwood]].

[[Raven Blackwood|raven]] had lived for more than a century, yet he looked no older than twenty-four. he had messy black hair, pale skin, and eyes so dark that people rarely noticed the faint red hidden within them. he was tall and slim, with a quiet face that made him difficult to understand.

unlike other [[Vampire Lore|vampires]], [[Raven Blackwood|raven]] possessed a rare ability. during the day, he could take the appearance of a human. his sharp teeth disappeared, his ears returned to normal, and his eyes lost their unnatural glow. but the transformation was never complete. when night came, his disguise faded, his skin grew paler, his ears lengthened, and his red eyes returned. sunlight in his true form burned him.

[[Raven Blackwood|raven]] had been born into an old vampire family whose house stood near [[The Forest|the forest]]. his father, [[Alaric Blackwood|alaric blackwood]], believed humans existed to serve [[Vampire Lore|vampires]]. he considered compassion a weakness. his mother, [[Elena Blackwood|elena]], believed humans and [[Vampire Lore|vampires]] could live beside one another.

[[Raven Blackwood|raven]] had listened to her.

years ago, he helped a human family escape from his father's reach. [[Alaric Blackwood|alaric]] ordered [[Raven Blackwood|raven]] to kill them as punishment. [[Raven Blackwood|raven]] refused. his father tried to kill him instead, and [[Elena Blackwood|elena]] sacrificed herself to help her son escape. [[Raven Blackwood|raven]] survived, but lost the only person who had made him feel he could be something other than a monster.

since then, he had lived alone. he had learned that hiding was safer than hoping. but he was tired of surviving. he wanted to know what it felt like to live.

one morning, [[Raven Blackwood|raven]] left [[The Forest|the forest]] and walked toward [[Hollow Creek|hollow creek]].

the village appeared beyond a narrow road, with old stone houses, wooden shops, and chimneys sending thin trails of smoke into the sky. people carried baskets through the streets, children ran between houses, and shopkeepers arranged their goods outside their doors.

[[Raven Blackwood|raven]] stood at the entrance for a long time. then he adjusted the collar of his charcoal coat and stepped into the village.

his first problem was finding somewhere to stay.

he found a small [[The Boarding House|boarding house]] run by an elderly widow named [[Mrs Whitmore|mrs. whitmore]]. she studied him as he placed a few coins on the desk.

"you look like someone running away from something," she said.

"does everyone who rents a room have to explain their life story?" [[Raven Blackwood|raven]] asked.

"only the ones who look like trouble."

he had expected suspicion, perhaps fear, but not humor. almost against his will, he smiled.

"then i'll try not to look like trouble."

[[Mrs Whitmore|mrs. whitmore]] laughed and handed him a key.

his room was small, with a narrow bed, a wooden desk, and a window overlooking the street. it was more than enough. for the first time in years, [[Raven Blackwood|raven]] had a place he could call his own.

that afternoon, he walked through [[Hollow Creek|hollow creek]]. he discovered a [[The Bakery|bakery]] near the market, a [[The Church|church]] at the end of a narrow lane, and a [[The Public Square|public square]] where villagers gathered to exchange news. he also noticed how quickly their voices grew quiet whenever anyone mentioned [[The Forest|the forest]].

some spoke of missing travelers. others blamed strange illnesses and failed harvests on [[Vampire Lore|vampires]].

[[Raven Blackwood|raven]] knew some of their fears were based on real events. [[Vampire Lore|vampires]] had harmed humans, and his own father was proof that the stories were not entirely false. but people could be cruel without fangs, too.

as the sun disappeared behind the rooftops, [[Raven Blackwood|raven]] returned to his room. his disguise faded, and he checked the door twice before sitting at his desk.

he touched the old [[The Silver Ring|silver ring]] he had inherited from his mother.

he remembered her words: *being different does not make you evil, [[Raven Blackwood|raven]]. what matters is what you choose to do with the life you've been given.*

tomorrow, he would try again.

he did not know that somewhere in the same village, a young woman named [[Iris|iris]] was spending her evening arguing with a book that refused to stay closed. nor did he know she would soon become the one person he could never bring himself to forget.

for now, he was only a stranger in town. and for the first time in a very long while, [[Raven Blackwood|raven blackwood]] had something to look forward to.
