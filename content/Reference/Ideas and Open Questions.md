# ideas and open questions

## decided
- title: *Raven Blackwood*
- protagonist: Raven Blackwood
- love interest: Iris
- setting: Hollow Creek, a village near a dark forest
- genre: gothic vampire romance
- romance: teasing, arguments, unexpected tenderness, gradual emotional growth
- ending: Iris dies after the fire; Raven leaves alone and remembers the house they never found.

## possible revisions
- establish the exact historical period.
- decide whether Iris's grandmother's old books contain clues about Raven's family.
- expand the hunters' role or clarify the village itself organizes the persecution.
- add more scenes of Raven and Iris's ordinary life before the tragedy.
- decide whether survivors later tell the story of Hollow Creek.
